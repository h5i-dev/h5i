Build and Verify Your Web Apps.

h5i extracts the kernel of a Rust web app into Lean 4 and proves its rules. In this board app, the theorem authorized shows that every write the kernel makes is allowed. Only a post's author or a moderator can delete it, whatever the request.

The same approach reaches embedded and edge devices. In the thermostat example, h5i proves the HTTP/1.1 parser and the kernel, including an interlock that keeps the heater off at 35 °C and above, and serves them with no OS and no heap on an STM32F407 or a XIAO RP2040.

The Rust function and Lean theorems on screen come from examples/app/board. The terminal output, the attacker, and their requests are illustrations. So are the robot vacuum, smart speaker and server rack, and the green marks they get at the end. They show where h5i is meant to be used, and the film does not claim these products run h5i today. The proof covers the kernel's writes. It does not cover HTTP routes that bypass the kernel.
